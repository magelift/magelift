# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, sexualized attention, trolling, insults, threats, doxxing, and other
conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method published in the repository's Security tab. Maintainers will respect
the reporter's privacy and respond proportionately, including correction, warning,
temporary exclusion, or permanent removal. Maintainers who do not follow this code
may be removed by the governance process.
